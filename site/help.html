How it works

Where the questions come from

This site collects two free daily practice questions for the NAVLE:

Zuku NAVLE
Zuku's "NAVLE Question of the Day". Every question back to January 2024 is here, each with its answer and explanation.
Dose of VetPrep
VetPrep's "Dose of VetPrep" daily question, starting in late October 2026, with its answer and explanation.

When new questions show up

Both send a new question every weekday (Monday to Friday), and none on weekends. New questions are added to this site on their own during the day. You don't need to do anything.

A new VetPrep question can show up before its answer does. If you answer it before then, you'll see "Answer not saved yet" and a Check the answer on VetPrep link that opens VetPrep's own page for that question. The answer is usually added here later the same day.

Answering a question

Tap the answer you think is right. The correct answer turns green; if you picked a different one, yours turns red. The explanation appears underneath. Tap Next for another question.

If a question has a picture, tap it to see it full screen, and tap again to close it. The original link at the top of a Zuku question opens that question on Zuku's website.

The two menus at the top

The first menu picks which questions you see: All sources, only Zuku NAVLE, or only Dose of VetPrep.

The second menu picks which questions come next:

Due
The best one for everyday studying. It first brings back questions you've answered before that are ready for another look, then gives you new ones, newest first. Questions you get right come back less and less often (the next day, then after 3 days, a week, two weeks, a month, two months). A question you get wrong comes back soon, until you get it right.
Unseen
Only questions you haven't answered yet.
Missed
Only questions you got wrong the last time you answered them.
Newest first
Every question in order, starting with the most recent.
Random
Every question in a shuffled order.

Whatever you pick, your answers still count toward the Due schedule.

The numbers under the title

questions
How many questions there are for the source you picked.
seen
How many of them you've answered at least once.
due
How many you've answered before that are ready to review now.
% correct
How often you've picked the right answer, counting every try.

Your progress

Your progress is saved in the web browser you're using, on that device. Your phone and your computer each keep their own progress, and friends you share the site with keep theirs.

Using a private or incognito window, or clearing your browser's history and website data, starts your progress over.
